# Prismwing: The Return of Solas

Fly the Prismwing through seven colour worlds, burn back Anmorolc's acid storms, beat each world's boss and bring the seven Prism Shards home.

Play: https://thespectrumtechengine.com/prismwing/

- Drag to fly (hold still to fly level). Double tap, or tap with your other thumb, to fire.
- On a computer: up and down arrows to fly, Space to fire, P to pause.
- Beat all seven worlds to win, then keep flying in a harder round.

The Android app (android-app/) opens the live game; every push builds a signed APK release.
