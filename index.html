PRISMWING

The Return of Solas

Anmorolc has drained the light of Solas from the universe and shattered it into seven Prism Shards. Fly the Prismwing through seven colour worlds, defeat each world's boss and bring the light home, Corcra.

How to play

Drag to fly; hold still to fly level. Double tap, or tap with your other thumb, to fire. Shoot acid clouds to burn them back into light; Dark Red clouds take 2 shots. Every cloud you clear fills the sky with your world's colour, and a full sky completes the level. Each colour world has 3 levels, and the third is a boss guarding that colour's Prism Shard. Enemy jets fire as they arrive, so keep moving or shoot their bullets down. Downed pilots eject: cut their strings with your laser or fly through them. Acid rain eats your hull, which repairs once you're out of the rain. Hits in a row build a combo multiplier. On a computer: ↑ and ↓ to fly, Space to fire, P to pause.

Points: cloud 5, red cloud 10, jet 15 to 25, cut strings 20, snagged pilot 30, level bonus 50 × level.

Your best score stays on this device. To help improve the game, it counts a few anonymous things, like how many flights are started. Help or found a bug? support@thespectrumtechengine.com
